# Contributing

This project is seeking adversarial review, not agreement by default.

Useful contributions include:

- a bounded counterexample to a classification prerequisite;
- a case where the proposed use of `UNKNOWN`, `CONFLICTED`, or `REFUTED` hides an
  important distinction;
- a clearer falsification condition;
- evidence that a term duplicates or conflicts with established literature;
- an experimental design that distinguishes a deceptive bridge from a simpler
  explanation; or
- a correction to a citation, source status, or claimed relationship with VSTD.

## Case safety

Do not submit private evidence, personal data, confidential records, or unsupported
accusations about identifiable people or organizations. Prefer synthetic or
already-public cases and bind every factual claim to an exact public source. Applying
the taxonomy is not permission to label a real subject deceptive.

## Pull requests

1. Create a focused branch.
2. Keep the contribution Markdown-first unless a separately discussed implementation
   has a demonstrated need.
3. State the claim or rule being changed.
4. Add a counterexample or synthetic assessment when the change affects a
   classification boundary.
5. State what evidence would falsify or materially qualify the proposed rule.
6. Check every internal and external link.
7. Do not claim VSTD conformance, adoption, validation, or frozen compatibility.

Changes to `UNKNOWN`/`CONFLICTED` handling or the deceptive-bridge prerequisite need
especially explicit justification because they can silently strengthen allegations.
